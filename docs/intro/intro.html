拼接髮檢查

Spliced-hair check
STUDIO + MAKER
用網格與射線量測，數據化判斷拼接是否合理；agent 可以「調整 → 量測 → 再調整」直到達標。
Geometry and raycasts turn "does the hair look right" into numbers the agent can iterate on.
hair_check · coverage
頭頂、後腦、左右的頭皮覆蓋率；瀏海另計，耳朵自動排除
Scalp coverage for top, back and sides; forehead separate, ears excluded
gaps · candidates
每個缺口列出最近的三塊髮片與換算好的建議位移
Each gap lists the three closest pieces with ready-made moves
pieces · covers
每塊髮片覆蓋多少頭皮，前後比較就知道哪一塊跑掉；浮起判斷
Scalp covered per piece reveals the one that slipped; floating check
consistency
髮片顏色與光澤是否一致，列出顏色或光澤不同的格位
Flags pieces whose color or gloss differs from the rest

髮片自動遮罩

Automatic hair alpha mask
STUDIO + MAKER
髮片位置滿意但尾部太長穿模？agent 自動找出穿進身體的位置，只遮掉穿出去的那段，存進卡片。
Happy with a hair piece but its tail clips? The agent finds where it goes into the body and hides only the part beyond it.
hair_mask_preview · tail
沿著髮絲從髮根找第一次穿進身體／服裝的位置，遮掉之後到髮尾；也有 length、inside、below、region
Follows each strand from its root and trims from the first crossing; also length, inside, below, region
sharedPercent · overlay
檢查貼圖是否與其他髮絲共用，避免誤遮；UV 疊圖與 3D 標記預覽
Flags texture shared with other strands; UV overlay and 3D markers
hair_mask_apply · revert
寫進 shader 的 _AlphaMask，透過 Material Editor 存卡；可立即還原。也可改用 bones_set 縮短髮尾骨骼
Written to _AlphaMask via Material Editor; instant revert; or shorten tail bones
